Color Converter — HEX, RGB, HSL and HSV

Convert a colour between HEX, RGB, HSL and HSV, preview it, and check whether white or black text stays readable on it.

The four ways to write the same colour

  • HEX — #2563eb. Six hexadecimal digits, two per channel. Found in CSS, design tools and brand guidelines. The shorthand #2af means #22aaff.
  • RGB — rgb(37, 99, 235). Each channel runs 0–255. Directly matches how screens store colour.
  • HSL — hsl(221, 83%, 53%). Hue in degrees, saturation and lightness in percent. The most useful form for adjusting a colour: to make a lighter tint you raise L, to mute it you drop S.
  • HSV / HSB — hsv(221, 84%, 92%). Same hue, but value instead of lightness. This is the model behind most colour pickers, because it maps cleanly onto a square: hue around the edge, saturation across, value down.

Why HSL is worth learning

Editing a palette by HEX is guesswork — changing one character of #2563eb to get a lighter blue gives you a different hue as often as not. In HSL, a tint is "same H and S, higher L", and a shade is "same H and S, lower L". Whole design systems are built this way, and it is why generated palette tools always work in HSL rather than RGB.

Contrast, and why it is a legal matter

WCAG sets the standard: normal body text needs a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1, large text (18pt, or 14pt bold) at least 3:1. Government and public-sector sites are obliged to meet it in many countries, and it is the single most common accessibility failure on the web. The ratio is computed from relative luminance, not from how different the colours look — which is why a saturated yellow can be unreadable with white text while a mid grey is fine.

The formula used here is the one from the WCAG specification, so the numbers match the official checkers.

FAQ

Why does the same hex look different on two monitors?

Uncalibrated displays, different colour gamuts (sRGB vs Display P3) and different brightness settings all shift the appearance. The numeric value is identical; the rendering is not.

Is there a difference between HSV and HSB?

No — they are two names for the same model. Photoshop calls it HSB; most programming libraries call it HSV.

Does this handle 8-digit hex like #2563ebff?

The 8-digit form adds an alpha channel. This converter works on the six-digit form; strip the last two digits, or convert the alpha yourself as a percentage.
